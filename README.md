## Overview

Systems Architect and Backend Engineer who translates complex operational bottlenecks into scalable, production-ready software. With 2+ years of experience engineering B2B and public-sector platforms—spanning healthcare logistics, enterprise HR payroll, financial ledgers, and municipal utility CRMs—I build high-concurrency, decoupled backends grounded in **Domain-Driven Design (DDD)**.

- **Backend Architecture:** Modular, event-driven, and stateless services engineered with **NestJS**, **TypeScript**, **Python**, **FastAPI**, and **Django**.
- **Database as the Vault:** ACID-compliant relational systems using **PostgreSQL** and **Supabase**—leveraging Row-Level Security (RLS), PL/pgSQL stored procedures, advisory locks, and write-only audit triggers—paired with **Redis** for low-latency caching, queues, and session state.
- **Telemetry & Production Ops:** Full-lifecycle ownership from stakeholder discovery to zero-downtime CI/CD (**Docker**, **Kamal**, **GitHub Actions**) and end-to-end observability (**OpenTelemetry**, **Prometheus**, **Grafana**, **Sentry**).
